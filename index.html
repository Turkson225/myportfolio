ENGINEERING PROCESS

From a rough idea to a testable system.

The process is iterative. Findings from testing often loop back into hardware, firmware and interface decisions.

01IdeaDefine the problem and intended user.
02ResearchStudy constraints, components and system risks.
03SimulationValidate logic and architecture before hardware.
04HardwareWire, assemble and establish power integrity.
05FirmwareImplement sensing, state logic and communication.
06DashboardTurn telemetry into useful operator information.
07TestingExercise normal, edge and failure scenarios.
08DocumentationCapture architecture, results and operation.
09DeploymentPublish, demonstrate and prepare the next revision.
